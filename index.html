Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before you get started. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our team →

How do I access the courses after enrolling?

Immediately after enrollment, you'll receive a welcome email with your account credentials. Sign in to the Corso dashboard and your full curriculum is unlocked — including all on-demand videos, downloadable resources, and the community space.

Are the courses self-paced or scheduled?

Both. Every on-demand module is self-paced with lifetime access. On top of that, we run weekly live workshops and monthly expert seminars that you can attend live or watch the replay.

Can I download videos for offline viewing?

Yes — our iOS and Android apps support offline downloads for all enrolled courses. Just tap the download icon on any video and it's available for 30 days, even without an internet connection.

Do you offer certificates of completion?

Yes. After finishing any course track, you'll receive a verifiable digital certificate you can add to your LinkedIn profile, share with your team, or include in your portfolio. Each certificate has a unique URL for verification.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked refund on all annual plans. If Corso isn't the right fit, email us within 30 days of your purchase and we'll process a full refund — usually within 2 business days.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The 7-day free trial gives you full access to one complete course track plus the community space. No credit card required to start, and you can cancel anytime before the trial ends.

Do you offer team or enterprise plans?

Absolutely. Our enterprise plans include SSO, custom learning paths, dedicated success managers, and volume pricing for teams of 10+. Reach out to our sales team for a tailored proposal and demo.
